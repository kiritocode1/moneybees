## 7. Complaints (priority)
Answer. Two clocks apply.
- SCORES-routed complaints: Action Taken Report (ATR) within 21 calendar days of receipt. SCORES circular para 1.2, 1.3 (p.~3). [observed] "Entities shall resolve the Complaint and upload the ATR on SCORES within 21 calendar days of receipt of the Complaint." Effective 1 Apr 2024 (extension; secondary source). Review path: complainant may seek first review within 15 calendar days of the ATR (para 2.1); second review within 15 calendar days of the Designated Body's ATR (para 3.1); if still unsatisfied after SEBI review, ODR (para 9, Annexure text: "shall seek appropriate remedies through the Online Dispute Resolution mechanism"). Investor may file within one year of the cause of action (para 3 of the annexure). [observed]
- Direct complaints, PMS: redressal "Within 30 days, subject to all the information required ... is provided by the complainant". PMS MC Annexure 4B, Part C item 30, p.84. Note 1 on p.84: days are "clear working days". [observed] PMS MC has no acknowledgement timeline (grep "acknowledg" gives only agreement acknowledgement at line 1795). [observed absence]
- Direct and SCORES complaints, AIF: "Within 21 days from the date of receipt of complaint" for both direct response and SEBI/SCORES redressal. AIF MC Annexure 4 (Investor Charter) Part D item 3, p.99. [observed]
- No acknowledgement deadline found in any of the three documents. Do not state one in templates. [observed absence]
- What the investor is told: SCORES registration is mandatory (PMS Annexure 4B D.a, p.85); investor relations officer name, address, phone in the Disclosure Document (D.b); grievance and dispute mechanism in the DD (D.c); SEBI address Office of Investor Assistance and Education, SEBI Bhavan, Plot No. C4-A, G Block, BKC, Bandra (E), Mumbai 400 051 (D.e, same in AIF Annexure 4 E.4, p.100). AIF disputes go to "mediation and/or conciliation and/or arbitration" (AIF Annexure 4 E.2, p.100). PMS fee disputes go to arbitration (PMS MC 7.1.2, p.86). [observed]
- Smart ODR: neither master circular mentions ODR (grep SMART ODR / ODR gives 0 hits in both). The SCORES circular says ODR is the next step after SEBI review. The name "Smart ODR" and the portal were not read. [unverified]. Template line may say "If unresolved after review on SCORES, you may use the Online Dispute Resolution mechanism for the securities market", without a URL until verified.
- Website duty (not email): PMS monthly complaints table by the 7th (PMS MC 7.2.1, p.86). AIF complaints data quarterly as a PPM chapter (AIF MC 2.3.3, 2.3.4, from earlier notes).
Template implication: footer block with IRO name/phone/email, direct-complaint route, SCORES link (scores.sebi.gov.in), and the line that unresolved complaints can go to SCORES then ODR. Time promises: PMS "within 30 working days of receiving all information from you" (and 21 calendar days if raised on SCORES); AIF "within 21 days". Status: observed.

## 5. AIF investor reporting (priority)
Source: AIF MC Annexure 4, Investor Charter, Part C and D, pp.97 to 99. [observed]
- Valuation: Cat I and II at least every six months. Cat III is not shown in the lines I read. [unverified for Cat III valuation frequency; read p.98-99 fully before finalising]
- NAV disclosure for Cat III: "Close ended fund - quarterly basis; Open ended fund - monthly basis" (item 1.b, p.98). Flyingbee's structure (open or close ended) must be confirmed from its PPM. [observed rule; applicability inferred]
- Material risks disclosure (concentration, FX, leverage, realisation, strategy, reputation, ESG): "Category III - within 60 days from the end of the quarter end or earlier as per the fund documents" (item 2.b, p.99). [observed]
- Financial, risk management, operational, portfolio and transactional information: "To be disclosed periodically to the investors" (item 2.c). Fees ascribed to Manager or Sponsor and fees charged to the fund or investee companies are also disclosed (item 2.d). [observed]
- Legal inquiries, material liability, PPM breach: "As and when occurred" (e-g). Conflict of interest: "As and when they arise or seem likely to arise" (h). [observed]
- Any change in PPM or fund documents: "On consolidated basis within one month of end of each Financial Year" (item 2.i, p.99). [observed]
- Material change needing investor consent: change of sponsor or manager, change in control, change in fee structure or hurdle rate resulting in higher fees (AIF MC 13.1.1, p.51). Dissenting holders get an exit option, not less than one month to dissent; exit completed within 3 months of the dissent window (13.1.1 (i), (v), p.51-52). Exit not needed if 75 percent by value approve for (a) and (b) (13.1.2). Investor Charter also lists consent for material changes to terms of PPM such as investment strategy, and winding up before tenure (Part C item 2.3, 2.4, p.97). [observed]
- Indicative NAV disclaimer, AIF MC 11.4.4, p.47, exact text (addressed to depositories, so applying it to Moneybee emails is inferred): "Net Asset Value (NAV) being shown is on the basis of valuation methodology and accounting practice followed by your respective AIF. This is an indicative NAV. For more details, please refer to your statement of accounts and fund documents." [observed text]
- Benchmark rule, AIF MC 22.5.2, p.79: "In any reporting to the existing investors, if performance of the AIF/Scheme is compared to any benchmark, a copy of the performance versus benchmark report provided by the Benchmarking Agency shall also be provided". 22.5.1: same for the PPM and any marketing material that mentions past performance. Benchmarking data to agencies is half-yearly (22.4, earlier notes). [observed] Practical rule: an investor email that shows Flyingbee returns against any benchmark must attach the agency report; an email without a benchmark comparison does not trigger 22.5.2 (inferred).
- Annual report timeline (AIF Regs reg 23, annual audited accounts within 180 days of year end), quarterly Cat III report to investors: not in the master circular text I read; the Regulations were not reached. [unverified]. Investor Charter says Cat I/II risk disclosure is within 180 days of year end, so 180 days is probable for the annual report but is not confirmed for Cat III.
- Valuation and NAV also flow to depositories and RTA (CAMS): AIF MC 11.4.3 (NAV upload by RTAs). Emails should not contradict the uploaded NAV. [inferred]
Template implication: quarterly investor letter fields: NAV per unit and date, scheme name and SEBI reg, indicative-NAV sentence, valuation basis, material-risk section (60 days after quarter end), fees section, conflicts/legal items if any, benchmark report attachment if any benchmark appears, how to reach statement of accounts. Status: observed, with the Cat III valuation frequency, annual report timeline and reg text unverified.

## 2. PMS quarterly report, Annexure 5D (priority)
Rule: PMS MC 5.5.1, p.54: report "in the format provided at Annexure 5D ... to their clients on a quarterly basis", which includes related-party investments (5.5.1.1), passive breach with steps taken (5.5.1.2), credit ratings of debt and hybrid (5.5.1.3), and the fee-calculation annexure in APMI format (5.5.1.4). No day-count deadline for sending the quarterly client report is stated in the paragraphs read. [observed absence] Annexure 5D is on pp.153 to 159. Sections, in order [observed]:
- Header: "Account Statement for Quarter ended ___", PM email and telephone, Name of Distributor ("Direct Plan" if none).
- A. Account Overview: client name, PAN, address, email, phone, unique client code, account activation date, type of PMS, investment approach, benchmark, amount managed under the approach, percent AUM under the approach. Notes: separate reports per approach; joint holder details; "for any request for change of facts ... get in touch with [Email ID] or [Phone Number]".
- B. Portfolio Details: allocation table (Equity, Plain Debt, Structured Debt, Equity Derivatives, Commodity Derivatives, Goods, Mutual Funds, Cash and equivalent, Other Assets; purchase value, market value, percent of AUM). Portfolio Summary: value at start and end of quarter; capital inflow, outflow, interest, dividend, other income, management fee, performance fee, expenses at actuals, other expenses, realised and unrealised gain/loss, commission paid to distributor, brokerage paid.
- C. Performance (p.156): disclaimer "Performance data for Portfolio Manager and Investment Approach provided hereunder is not verified by any regulatory authority." Table 1Y, 3Y, 5Y, 10Y, since inception: returns of client portfolio, aggregate returns of the Investment Approach, benchmark performance. TWRR; all investments incl. cash.
- D. Transaction details: capital contribution since inception (date, inflow, outflow); investments during the period (security, date, buy/sell, quantity, gross rate, net rate, net value; net-rate calculation clarified); holding report at quarter end (quantity, average cost, market rate, total cost, market value, percent of portfolio).
- E. Other important information: deviations from the investment approach; delayed coupons; defaults; collateral held for derivatives; other assets outstanding over 3 months; related-party investments (transaction-wise and security-wise); passive breaches (date, steps, date rectified, whether within 90 days); credit ratings; other important information.
Plus 5.6.2, p.55 [observed]: when reporting performance to an investor, show XIRR per investment approach with min, max and median XIRR across all investors in the approach, TWRR and benchmark trailing returns shown separately, with this mandatory disclaimer: "Please note that performance of your portfolio may vary from that of other investors and that generated by the Investment Approach across all investors because of 1) the timing of inflows and outflows of funds; and 2) differences in the portfolio composition because of restrictions and other constraints." (Quote is the start of the text; the line break "restrictions and other constraints" is complete.)
Template implication: the email is a cover note; the PDF must carry sections A to E. Cover email fields: quarter end, client code, approach, benchmark, link or attachment, change-of-facts contact line. Status: observed.

## 8. Marketing emails to prospects
- PMS Annexure 2A, pp.67-68 (code of advertisement). "Advertisement" includes "circulars ... or through any electronic media", so an email campaign is covered. [observed] Rules: truthful, fair, clear (1.1); no exaggerated picture or implication that gains will repeat (1.2); no info dependent on assumptions (1.6); no promise or guarantee of assured or fixed return (1.7); no comparison with another PM unless fair and complete (1.8); content limited to scheme particulars (1.4). Breach is a breach of the PM code of conduct (2.1).
- PMS performance in marketing: net of all fees and expenses including taxes (4.5.3.2, p.43); same as reported to SEBI (4.5.3.4); firm level equals combined portfolios (4.5.3.5); disclaimer "performance related information provided therein is not verified by SEBI" in all marketing material (4.5.3.6, p.43). TWRR with trailing benchmark return (5.6.1, p.54); relative performance vs benchmark and vs other PMs in the Strategy (5.6.4, p.55-56); no model-portfolio returns or cherry-picked investor returns (5.6.3). Applies to any entity publishing performance (5.6.7, p.56). Direct onboarding option must be prominent in marketing material (2.3.2, p.20). [observed]
- AIF MC 3.2.1, p.18: marketing documents "distributed only on a private basis to its proposed investors and are in accordance with the PPM". 3.2.2: PPM provided before commitment, with acknowledgement of receipt. 3.2.3: no assured returns. 22.5.1: past performance requires the benchmarking agency report. [observed] So a cold mass mailer for Flyingbee is not allowed; email only identified proposed investors who have enquired or are eligible, and attach no performance without the agency report. [inferred]
- DPDP / consent for unsolicited email: not read. Earlier notes (DPDP Rules 2025, main duties from about 13 May 2027) are secondary. SEBI has no email-consent rule in either master circular text (not grepped exhaustively for "consent" outside 75 percent items). [unverified]
- Required disclaimers on any prospect email: not verified by SEBI line (PMS), no assured returns, past performance not indicative of future (this exact phrase is not mandated in Annexure 2A; inferred from 1.2), registration number INP000001959, direct onboarding option, for AIF "private placement, eligible investors only". Status: observed for the PMS items, inferred for the rest.

## 1. PMS onboarding
Source: PMS MC Annexure 4B Part C table, pp.80-84 [observed]; 4.3 fee annexure pp.39-40.
- Latest Disclosure Document provided to the investor prior to account opening, and on the website at all times (item 28, p.84). Items 7, 9 to 12, 17, 18, 20, 22, 23, 24: SEBI registration number, type of account (discretionary), what a discretionary account entails and PM powers, termination conditions, fees and payment mode/frequency, PoA scope, risks, indicative tenure, client restrictions/negative list: "At the time of client signing the agreement; this information should be a part of the account opening form" (pp.81-83). Net worth: in the DD whenever material change (item 8).
- Fees annexure in the client agreement (4.3.1, p.39): separate annexure, sample portfolio Rs 50 lakh over one year, three scenarios (+20 percent, -20 percent, unchanged), Annexure 4A. For clients onboarded on or after 1 Oct 2024 with a performance fee: also one-year and multi-year illustrations with high watermark, APMI standard format (4.3.2). Font at least 11 (4.3.3). Client separately signs the fees annexure with a handwritten or typed note that they understood it (4.3.4, p.40). [observed]
- Direct onboarding option prominent in DD, marketing and website; no charges except statutory for direct clients (2.3.2, 2.3.3, p.20). [observed]
- After opening: custodian name and demat account number within 3 days of PMS and demat account opening (item 16); details of bank accounts where client funds are kept within 3 days (item 29, p.84); KRA and CKYC upload within 10 days (item 6, p.81); account opening 14 days from receipt of all requisite documents for non-individuals and NRIs (items 2, 3; individual timing is on the preceding page, not captured here). Copy of executed agreement and of PoA within 3 days of client request (items 13, 19). Notes: all day counts are clear working days (p.84). [observed]
- Quarterly: funds and securities balance statement and transaction details, "not later than on a quarterly basis" (items 15, 25, 26). Audited reports annually (item 21).
Template implication: welcome email with attachments or links to DD, agreement, fees annexure; second email within 3 working days of account opening with custodian, demat and bank details; response template for agreement copy requests within 3 days. Status: observed (individual account-opening timeline not read).

## 3. PMS performance wording and form
Covered in sections 8 and 2 above. Summary [observed]: TWRR, net of all fees and expenses including taxes, trailing benchmark return alongside, relative performance vs benchmark and vs other PMs in the Strategy in marketing, secondary benchmark treated the same (earlier notes), same numbers as reported to SEBI, disclaimer "not verified by SEBI" (4.5.3.6) and in the client report "not verified by any regulatory authority" (Annexure 5D). Change of investment approach that affects performance must be disclosed in marketing (4.5.3.3). Investment approach naming uniform across all communications (4.6.1, p.43). Strategy tags only; no other categorisation (5.6.3.1).

## 4. PMS fees
- No upfront fees, directly or indirectly (6.1.3.1, p.58). Brokerage at actuals; operating expenses excluding brokerage not above 0.50 percent per annum of average daily AUM (6.1.3.3); associate charges capped at 20 percent by value per associate per service (6.1.3.4). [observed]
- High water mark (6.1.3.6, p.59): "the highest value that the portfolio/account has reached"; value taken on the date performance fees are charged; "the frequency shall not be less than quarterly"; fee only on increase above the previous high water mark; adjusted proportionately for interim contributions or withdrawals (6.1.3.9, p.60); applies to discretionary and non-discretionary (6.1.3.8). [observed]
- Fee statement content: quarterly report lists management fee, performance fee, expenses at actuals, other expenses, distributor commission, brokerage in the Portfolio Summary (Annexure 5D); fee-calculation annexure in APMI format (5.5.1.4). Hurdle rate appears in the Annexure 4A illustration (hurdle 10 percent, performance fee 20 percent of profit over hurdle; illustrative values, not mandated rates). The APMI format itself was not obtained. [observed for rules; unverified for APMI layout]
- When fees can be charged: the text says "the frequency shall not be less than quarterly". The wording is ambiguous. The likely intent is that performance fees are charged at intervals of at least one quarter (never more often). The agreement fixes the actual frequency (item 18). [observed text, reading inferred]

## 6. AIF drawdown and distribution notices
No paragraph in the AIF MC prescribes content or notice period for capital calls or distributions. Only mentions found: borrowing to meet a shortfall in drawdown amount for Cat I and II (14.1.2, p.~56); the Investor Charter says the AIF will "distribute returns to the investors as per the fund documents" (Part B). The notice period and content come from the PPM and contribution agreement. [observed absence; Cat III Regs not read]. Also 3.2.2 a fund cannot call capital from an investor who has not received the PPM acknowledgement (inferred), and line 943 of the text says no further drawdown from certain investors (context not read). Template implication: build fields from the PPM: amount, due date, bank account, unit/commitment balance, purpose, and for distributions gross, tax withheld, net, record date. Status: observed absence, inferred content.
