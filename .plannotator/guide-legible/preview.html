Start here

Everything in the admin hangs off the sidebar on the left. It has four groups. Each does one kind of work, and each screen in it leads to the part of the site or the desk it changes.

1 2 3 4
  1. 1
    Desk: the daily work with people
    • Today leads to what needs you now: enquiries waiting, overdue replies, fact sheets to approve.
    • Enquiries leads to every message from the Contact page, each with its reply clock.
    • People leads to everyone who has written in or invests, with their history.
    • Deals leads to each prospective investment and the stage it has reached.
  2. 2
    Website: what visitors see on moneybee.in
    • Pages leads to all eleven pages; Edit opens one beside the live page.
    • Team, Documents, Case studies, Openings lead to lists that several pages show.
    • Office details leads to the phone, email, address and map every page uses.
    • Guide is this page.
  3. 3
    Publishing: the numbers and files behind the site
    • Fact Sheets leads to each month's returns; compliance approves them before the site changes.
    • Products leads to the PMS and the AIF, their benchmark and whether returns may be shown.
    • Files leads to every PDF and picture uploaded.
  4. 4
    Settings: how the desk runs and who may do what
    • Playbooks, Email templates, Enquiry settings lead to how enquiries are answered.
    • Client accounts, Staff lead to who can sign in, to the portal and to this admin.
    • Audit log leads to every change and who made it.

Every screen

One entry per screen: a picture of it with its controls numbered, what it is for, and how to use it well. A screen's How to use this button lands on its entry.

Documents

Website › DocumentsOpen Documents

The cards on the Investor Centre page. A card with a PDF or web address links to it; one without says Available on request.

1 2 3 4
  1. 1
    How to use this

    Opens this entry in the Guide from the screen itself.

  2. 2
    Create New

    Adds a card. Pick its group: the four groups are fixed, because the menu and footer link to them.

  3. 3
    Drag to reorder

    The order here is the order on the page, within each group.

  4. 4
    File

    Amber No file means the card says Available on request. Open the card, drop in the approved PDF, set its date and Save: it is live straight away.
