- Cloud circular SEBI/HO/ITD/ITD_VAPT/P/CIR/2023/033, 6 Mar 2023, para 3 Applicability: stock exchanges, CCs, depositories, brokers, DPs, AMCs/MFs, QRTAs, KRAs. PMS and AIF NOT listed. [observed, sebi.gov.in attachdocs/mar-2023/1678112480131.pdf]
- 12-month glide path (to ~6 Mar 2024) for existing cloud users [observed]. Principle 3.iii: data/logs "reside/be processed within the legal boundaries of India" [observed].
- CSCRF circular SEBI/HO/ITD-1/ITD_CSC_EXT/P/CIR/2024/113, 20 Aug 2024 (attachdocs/aug-2024/1724326790365.pdf): addressed to all AIFs and all Portfolio Managers (sections 1.5, 16.1) [observed]. Annexure J = cloud framework. Supersedes earlier circulars.
- CSCRF Hosted Service spec 8.4: if data centre outside India, copy of RE data in readable form kept in India [observed]. Data classification: "Regulatory Data" (core/critical, investor communication via emails/chat, data required by SEBI law, data RE deems sensitive) must be stored/accessible within India; Box Item 10 "REs shall ensure that processing and storage of data is done within legal boundaries of India" [observed].
- Adoption dates: Jan 1 2025 / Apr 1 2025, later extended to 31 Aug 2025; AIF/PM size categories revised (Apr 2025, Aug 2025 technical specs circular SEBI/HO/ITD-1/ITD_CSC_EXT/P/CIR/2025/119) [secondary for dates, not read].
- SEBI FAQ Jun 2025 Q26: key management routed abroad may violate data sovereignty; "SEBI is doing an active consultation to finalise ... Data Localisation" [observed].
- Does a marketing lead / job application / public figure count? Enquiry emails and investor communications fall under "Regulatory Data" b. (communication between investors and REs); prospect leads, applicants, public figures are not named. [inferred] Public published figures: low sensitivity, not clearly covered [inferred].

## Q2 DPDP
- DPDP Rules 2025, G.S.R. 846(E), Gazette 13 Nov 2025 (meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2025/11/53450e6e5dc0bfa85ebd78686cadad39.pdf) [observed].
- Rule 1(2) rules 1,2,17-21 immediate; 1(3) Rule 4 (Consent Managers) one year (13 Nov 2026); 1(4) Rules 3, 5-16, 22, 23 at 18 months = 13 May 2027 (date arithmetic inferred) [observed].
- Rule 3 notice: standalone, plain language, itemised data, specific purposes, link/means to withdraw consent as easily as given, exercise rights, complain to Board [observed].
- Rule 6 safeguards: encryption/masking, access control, logs and monitoring, backups, retain logs+data one year for breach detection, processor contract clauses [observed].
- Rule 7: tell each affected person without delay; Board without delay, detailed report within 72h [observed].
- Rule 8: erasure timelines are in Third Schedule (classes: e-commerce, online gaming, social media; not us) [observed headings]; Rule 8(3) retain personal data + traffic logs minimum 1 year then erase unless other law requires longer [observed]. Erasure on purpose served / consent withdrawal comes from Act s.8(7) [unverified, Act not read].
- Rule 9: publish contact of DPO/responsible person [observed].
- Rule 15: transfer abroad allowed, subject to government orders on foreign States; negative list, none notified as of Aug 2026 per secondary sources [observed text, secondary status].
- MeitY Jan 2026 proposal to shorten timelines: not confirmed gazetted [secondary, unverified].
