# Homepage story, answered in the decks' words

## 1. Goal

The evolution page repeats four ideas across 18 blocks and tells them out of order. Much of the copy in between is ours, and some of it is mannered.
After this change the page asks nine questions in the order a prospective investor asks them. Each question is answered once, in wording lifted verbatim from the two presentations, and each keeps the component already built for that concept.
Proof: every body line on the page traces to a slide (kept in `lib/insights.ts` with its citation), no idea is answered twice, and a full scroll at 390 and 1440 shows the order below.

Sources: **P** = Group profile, April 2026. **A** = Flyingbee AIF presentation, August 2026. Wording is copied as printed, typos included, unless you say otherwise.
