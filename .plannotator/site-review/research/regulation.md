# Regulation research, PMS + Cat III AIF website and back office (2026-10-12). Status: partial

Method: downloaded and text-extracted the PMS Master Circular (SEBI/HO/IMD/IMD-POD-1/P/CIR/2025/104, 16 Jul 2025, 207 pp, APMI-hosted copy) and the NEW AIF Master Circular (HO/19/34/11(6)2025-AFD-POD1/I/12928/2026, issued 3 Jun 2026, updated 16 Jun 2026, 153 pp, NPS Trust-hosted copy). Page and para refs below are from those texts. Not read at sebi.gov.in directly (host copies; sebi.gov.in search pages did not return the PDFs).
Correction to earlier notes: AIF master circular of 7 May 2024 (2024/39) is superseded by the 3 Jun 2026 one. Marketing/benchmark paras are now 3.2.1 and 22.5 (old 4.3 and 16.3.6).

## Website must-have (PMS)
1. Investor Charter prominently on the website. PMS MC para 4.4 (Annexure 4B). [observed] Wording is "advised to ... prominently displaying"; treat as mandatory in practice. Build /investor-charter page.
2. Monthly complaints data table on the website, by the 7th of the next month, Annexure 7A columns: received from Directly from investors / SEBI (SCORES) / Other; pending at end of last month, received, resolved, total pending, pending >3 months, average resolution time (days). PMS MC 7.2.1 + Annexure 7A. [observed] Needs an archive of months; admin should have a monthly entry form with a "due 7th" reminder.
3. Link/option for clients to lodge a complaint directly with the PM, plus link to SCORES and the SCORES app. PMS MC 7.2.2. [observed] ("may be provided" for SCORES links; direct complaint option is "advised").
4. Latest Disclosure Document available on the website at all times; material change in related-party investment info updated in DD and uploaded within 7 calendar days. PMS MC 4.7.3 and Annexure 4B item 28. [observed]
5. Direct onboarding option (no distributor, no charges except statutory) disclosed prominently in DD, marketing material and website. PMS MC 2.3.2. [observed]
6. Performance, if shown: net of all fees and expenses incl. taxes; identical to what is reported to SEBI; firm-level equals combined portfolios; disclaimer "performance related information provided is not verified by SEBI"; TWRR with trailing benchmark return, and relative performance vs benchmark and vs other PMs in the Strategy in marketing material showing performance; secondary benchmark shown the same way as primary. PMS MC 4.5.3.2 to 4.5.3.6, 5.6.1, 5.6.4, 7.1.1.8 (from prior notes). [observed] Pull numbers from a single approved dataset (the maker-checker fact sheet) so site and SEBI filings cannot differ.
7. Investment approach names and descriptions uniform across all documents and the site. PMS MC 4.6.1. [observed]
8. Grievance/investor relations officer name, address, phone in the Disclosure Document (Annexure 4B D.b); SCORES registration mandatory (Annexure 4B D.a). [observed] Put on contact page as well. SMART ODR link: the PMS MC text contains no ODR reference (grep 0 hits). [observed absence] ODR master circular (SEBI/HO/OIAE/OIAE_IAD-3/P/CIR/2023/195, 20/28 Dec 2023) not read; including the SMART ODR link is cheap, recommended not shown mandatory for PMS. [secondary]
9. SEBI registration number, registered office, compliance officer: standard; PM Regs 2020 not read. [inferred]

## Website must-not
1. No promise or guarantee of assured/fixed return, directly or indirectly. PMS MC Annexure 2A para 1.7 (p.68); AIF MC 3.2.3. [observed]
2. No past-performance portrayal implying repetition; no exaggerated claims without qualifying statements; no statements dependent on assumptions (no projections or illustrative future returns). Annexure 2A 1.2, 1.3, 1.6. [observed]
3. No comparison with another PM unless fair and complete. Annexure 2A 1.8. [observed]
4. Publicity may contain only info in the PM's scheme particulars. Annexure 2A 1.4. [observed]
5. No model-portfolio returns or cherry-picked investor returns; benchmark changes not retro-fitted (prior notes 4.6A.5/6, 5.6.3). [observed]
6. Testimonials: PMS MC has no explicit testimonial rule (grep 0 hits for testimonial, influencer). Annexure 2A (truthful, fair, not misleading) is the governing test. Client testimonials with returns = high risk. [observed absence; conclusion inferred]
7. AIF: no PPM or scheme terms published openly; marketing documents "distributed only on a private basis to proposed investors" and consistent with the PPM. AIF MC 3.2.1. [observed] Fund-raising solicitation after PPM filing with SEBI: AIF MC 2.4.1 (30-day fast track; circulate PPM to investors). [observed]
8. AIF: no performance claim without the benchmarking agency "performance versus benchmark" report. AIF MC 22.5.1 (PPM and any marketing/promotional material); 22.5.2 for reports to existing investors. [observed] Benchmarking is half-yearly, pre-tax net, INR and USD (22.4). [observed]
9. Do not run AIF performance on the open site, and do not mix AIF and PMS on one performance chart. [inferred from 22.5.1 + Annexure 2A]

## Category III AIF on a public website
- Allowed (inferred, low risk): who Moneybee is, SEBI registration number of the AIF, strategy description in neutral terms, investor charter link, contact. Not allowed: PPM, terms, fees, unit price/returns, or "invest now" calls to the general public. Anything beyond a neutral teaser should sit behind enquiry gating with a "private placement, eligible investors only" notice. [inferred; basis AIF MC 3.2.1 and AIF Regs reg 11, reg 11 text not read]
- Investor Charter for AIFs: Annexure 4, disclosed in the PPM (AIF MC 2.3.2). [observed] Not required on website by the text. Publishing it on the site is harmless and consistent.
- Complaints data for AIFs: per scheme, quarterly (Mar/Jun/Sep/Dec) in Annexure 5 format, "as a separate chapter in the PPM"; compiled within 7 days of quarter end (AIF MC 2.3.3, 2.3.4). [observed] Not a website duty per the text; maintain it in admin and refresh the PPM chapter.
- Eligibility: minimum investment (Rs 1 crore, reg 10(c)) and accredited investor rules (AI-only schemes, Rs 25 lakh min for AIs in some structures, annual income Rs 1 crore + net worth Rs 5 crore criteria at 10.2.1): AIF MC chapter 10 read in part; Rs 1 crore figure NOT confirmed in the text read. [partly observed] Enquiry form should capture investor type and show an eligibility notice.
- Indicative NAV disclaimer applies wherever AIF NAV is shown: "Net Asset Value (NAV) being shown is on the basis of valuation methodology and accounting practice followed by your respective AIF. This is an indicative NAV. For more details, please refer to your statement of accounts and fund documents." AIF MC 11.4.4 (addressed to depositories/RTA channels). [observed text; applying it to Moneybee portal is inferred]

## Portal / back office must-have
1. Quarterly client report in Annexure 5D format within the cycle SEBI sets; content: account overview (client code, investment approach, benchmark, amount managed, distributor or "Direct Plan"), portfolio allocation, related-party investments, passive breach, credit ratings, fee calculation annexure in APMI format, TWRR vs benchmark. PMS MC 5.5.1, 5.6, Annexure 5D. [observed] Portal "statements" should be this report, not an ad hoc PDF.
2. Client service timelines from Annexure 4B: executed agreement copy within 3 days of request; custodian/demat details within 3 days of account opening; funds/securities balance statement quarterly or on request; transaction details quarterly or on request. [observed items 13 to 16, 26 to 29]
3. Data submitted to SEBI quarterly within 15 calendar days of quarter end (5.4.3) and monthly report (5.1). [observed] Back-office exports should reuse the same data.
4. Annual audit of firm-level performance, compliance confirmation to SEBI within 60 days of FY end; APMI standard ToR since 1 Oct 2023 (5.3). [observed, 60 days from secondary summary and 5.3.3.3]
5. Maker-checker for fact sheets and monthly complaints publication: matches 4.5.3.4 (website equals SEBI-reported). [inferred]
6. AIF: investor reports/quarterly activity report to SEBI (21.1.2) and benchmarking data to agencies within 45 days of each half-year (22.4). [observed] If the portal shows AIF performance to existing investors vs a benchmark, attach the benchmark agency report (22.5.2). [observed]
7. CSCRF applies to AIFs and PMs (SEBI/HO/ITD-1/ITD_CSC_EXT/P/CIR/2024/113, 20 Aug 2024; prior notes). Regulatory data (investor communications, statements) must stay in India. Vercel/Neon default regions are not India: use an India region for portal data and email logs. [observed in prior notes; mapping inferred]
8. DPDP Rules 2025 (G.S.R. 846(E), 13 Nov 2025): notice, consent, breach 72h, one-year log retention, DPO contact; main obligations commence about 13 May 2027 (prior notes). [observed in prior notes] Enquiry and careers forms need a standalone plain-language notice, itemised data, purposes, withdrawal route, retention period, erasure process.
9. Record keeping and audit trail: PM Regs 2020 and AIF Regs retention periods not read. [unverified] Keep immutable audit logs of publication approvals, statements issued, complaint entries.
