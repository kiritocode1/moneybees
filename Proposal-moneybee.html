Executive summary

Moneybee requires a website that performs in search, presents the firm with the authority its record supports, and allows the investment team to publish research without engineering assistance. This proposal treats the three as a single engagement, on the basis that each depends on the other two.

We recommend a purpose-built application on Next.js, deployed to Vercel. It comprises a public site scoped entirely to the two regulated products, and a private administration platform through which Moneybee maintains every part of that site. Search performance is engineered into the build during construction, and the resulting foundation transfers to Moneybee in full at handover, with no continuing retainer attached to it.

Moneybee / Context

01 Context and objectives

Three conditions define what this website is required to achieve. Each is addressed by an identified component of the build, and each is referenced again in the section that treats it in detail.

Search is where the enquiry begins. An investor considering where to place ₹50 lakh or more will ordinarily begin at a search engine. The managers who appear first are not necessarily the strongest; they are the managers whose sites were built with search in mind. Position for terms such as portfolio management services or alternative investment fund India is determined by site structure, page depth, crawlability and the frequency with which a firm publishes. A site of ten brochure pages offers a search engine very little to index, irrespective of the quality of the manager behind it. This is addressed in section 03.

The Fund is recently established. An AIF licensed in 2025 carries no extended performance record. An investor therefore assesses it as they would any new mandate: on the investment process, on the manner in which decisions are explained, and on whether the firm's commentary remains consistent across favourable and unfavourable periods. Evidence of that kind exists only where it has been written down, and it accrues to Moneybee only where it is published on moneybee.in. This is addressed in sections 04 and 05.

The two products serve different investors. SEBI sets the minimum investment at ₹50 lakh for portfolio management and ₹1 crore for an alternative investment fund. An investor holding ₹60 lakh is accordingly eligible for one product and not the other. Where a site does not make that distinction evident, the investor must establish it by telephone, and a proportion of them will not. This is addressed in section 06.

Why this is a single engagement. Search visibility, research publishing and product structure draw on one foundation. The pages that rank are the pages that explain the products, and the material that persuades an investor is the material that earns the search position. Commissioned separately, each is more expensive and less effective.
Moneybee / Audience

02 The target investor

The site is written for an investor holding ₹50 lakh or more in genuinely investible surplus who is deciding where to place it. Such an investor has met other managers and has encountered the standard vocabulary of the industry, market-beating returns, expert fund managers, long-term wealth creation, often enough that it no longer conveys information.

The question governing their reading of every page is narrower than performance, and it concerns trust rather than return.

Can this firm be trusted with a meaningful part of my family's wealth?

The table below sets out what bears on that question and what does not. The site is ordered so that the left column is answered before the visitor is asked for anything.

What decides itWhat does not
Downside protection, drawdown management, risk-adjusted returnsThe highest CAGR
A disciplined process that survives cyclesA view on where the market goes next
Clear fees, honest reporting, a reason each holding existsOne exceptional year
Alignment: managers with their own money invested, no churnSales enthusiasm
After-tax reality: turnover, LTCG, successionGross headline numbers
Access to the people making the decisionsA support ticket
Visible regulation, custodian and auditorLegal text at the foot of the page
The basis of the firm's credibility. Investors seldom leave a manager on account of returns alone. They leave following poor communication, undisclosed risk, drift from the stated strategy, overpromising, or an inability to explain a decision after the event. Moneybee is positioned to answer each of these, and the supporting material already exists: a career in Indian capital markets beginning in 1979, a firm established in 2004, senior personnel with fifteen, twenty and twenty five years of experience respectively, a transaction record including the sale of Zandu to Emami in 2008 and the Sun Pharmaceuticals public issue in 1995, and a regulatory standing that can be presented prominently. For a fund still establishing its record, this is the evidence an investor weighs in place of a long track record, and it is substantial.
Moneybee / Search

03 Search visibility

Search performance is treated as an engineering discipline and delivered as part of the build. The following components are included.

  • Keyword research report. Delivered in advance of implementation, a written report identifying the terms worth competing for, the search behaviour of investors and advisors, the relative difficulty of each term, and the page or article that should target it. This was requested in your brief, and it is the correct sequence.
  • Per page metadata. Title, description, canonical and Open Graph tags generated from the content model for every page and every article, so that no page can ship without them.
  • Structured data. JSON-LD covering Organization, FinancialProduct, Article, Person and JobPosting, enabling a search engine to interpret the nature of the firm, the products offered and the people responsible for them.
  • Technical foundation. Sitemap and robots directives generated automatically, canonical URLs, readable slugs, and server rendered HTML, which together render every page fully crawlable.
  • Core Web Vitals. Speed is a ranking factor. PageSpeed above 90 on mobile and desktop, and Largest Contentful Paint below 2.5 seconds, are treated as build requirements and verified before launch.
  • Measurement. Google Analytics 4 and Google Search Console configured and verified at launch, so that indexing and ranking gaps are visible from the first week of operation.
  • Answer engines. As contemplated by your scope statement, semantic HTML, structured data and consistently organised content allow an AI assistant to represent Moneybee accurately when a user asks it about portfolio management in India.
  • Transfer of ownership. The keyword report, the metadata model, the structured data and the verified Search Console property transfer to Moneybee at handover. The foundation continues to operate without further payment, and no retainer is attached to it.
A single subject, on a single domain. Every page of the site concerns portfolio management or alternative investments, so each reinforces the same subject and the terms Moneybee wishes to rank for are the terms the site genuinely addresses. The same discipline benefits the design, since a site with one subject can support a six item menu, generous spacing and large type. All published material remains on moneybee.in, since search authority accrues to the domain on which it is published and material hosted elsewhere builds another party's position.
Moneybee / Publishing

04 Research publishing

Investors assess a manager on the quality of its thinking, and search engines rank a site on the quality and consistency of what it publishes. A single activity satisfies both requirements, which is an unusual efficiency and one worth taking.

For the next several years, published research will be the most persuasive evidence available to the Fund.

A fund without an extended record is assessed on process, judgement and clarity, and published writing is where each of the three becomes visible. A sector note demonstrates how the firm reasons about a business. A quarterly letter demonstrates how a position was explained before its outcome was known, which is the one form of evidence that cannot be assembled retrospectively.

  • Sector notes. Short pieces addressing the sectors Moneybee researches directly, including the small and ultra-small capitalisation businesses that receive limited coverage elsewhere. These rank for specific terms and demonstrate depth at the same time.
  • Market commentary. Periodic commentary on conditions and positioning, in the firm's own voice, at whatever frequency the team can sustain.
  • Investor letters. Quarterly letters published where prospective investors may read them, allowing a reader to consider the reasoning and the outcome together.
  • Each piece as an indexed page. Every article receives its own URL, metadata, structured data and social card, generated automatically on publication.
The dependency on tooling. Publishing programmes fail where publishing is difficult. The administration platform described in section 05 is included so that an article is published in minutes, reaches the subscriber list the same day, and requires no one outside the Moneybee team.

Moneybee / Products

06 Product architecture

The two products fall under different regulations, carry different structures and disclosure obligations, and are available to different investors. SEBI sets the minimum investment for each, and that minimum determines the investors to whom each product may be offered at all.

Portfolio Management Services

₹50 lakh

Minimum investment, set by SEBI

  • Securities held in the investor's own name
  • Portfolio visible holding by holding
  • Discretionary mandate, disclosed fee structure
  • Returns reported against a stated benchmark

Alternative Investment Fund

₹1 crore

Minimum investment, set by SEBI

  • Pooled structure with a defined strategy and term
  • Governed by the fund's placement memorandum
  • Different fee and distribution mechanics
  • A narrower, larger set of eligible investors
The rationale for separate treatment. An investor holding ₹60 lakh qualifies for one product and not the other, and should be able to establish that within seconds, from the site, without a telephone call and without being directed along a path unavailable to them. An investor with several crore may wish to consider both, and should be able to compare them on an equivalent basis. Each product accordingly receives its own pages, its own performance presentation and its own disclosures, and the navigation makes the distinction apparent at the first step.
Moneybee / Compliance

07 Compliance and disclosure

For a SEBI registered manager, the disclosure material is the section of a website that a serious investor reads with the greatest care. Presented properly it contributes more to trust than any statement on the homepage, and this build treats it accordingly.

  • Investor and Regulatory Centre. A single destination holding the disclosure documents, the placement memorandum, the Investor Charter, complaint data and statutory notices, summarised in a concise footer on every page and accessible from anywhere on the site.
  • Registration presented prominently. SEBI registration numbers, category, and exchange or depository memberships set out at full size and high on the page.
  • Performance presented analytically. Returns against the appropriate benchmark across standard periods, together with rolling returns, drawdowns and a statement of calculation methodology, so that an advisor may verify the figures rather than accept them.
  • Fees in full. Management fee, performance fee, hurdle rate and high water mark presented in a plain table, with the worked example on a ₹50 lakh portfolio that regulation requires.
  • Grievance procedure. Named principal officer and investor relations contact, the complaint procedure, and links to the SCORES and ODR platforms.
  • Structured for approval. Each page carrying regulated content is constructed so that your compliance officer may review it as a single unit, and so that the text may subsequently be amended without engineering assistance.
Sequencing. Compliance review proceeds in parallel with the build. Templates and structure are submitted for approval early, so that final approval concerns content within a layout already agreed. This is what protects the delivery date.
Moneybee / Design

08 Visual direction and design system

Restrained, confident and quiet: generous spacing, large type and minimal movement. The intention is that a visitor should conclude that this firm has managed capital carefully for a long period before reading a sentence of the copy.

  • A design system. A single consistent specification of typography, colour, spacing and components, so that every page reads as the work of one firm and each additional page costs hours instead of days.
  • Typography as the primary element. One modern typeface used at genuine scale. Long form sections are set editorially, with appropriate measure and leading, because they are written to be read.
  • Disciplined use of colour. Black and near black for structure, a warm off white ground, and the Moneybee yellow reserved for accents and a single call to action in any view.
  • Component library. Strategy cards, performance tables, article layouts, team profiles, disclosure blocks and enquiry forms, recombined as the site develops.
  • Responsive across devices. Desktop, tablet and mobile, with particular attention to mobile, where the majority of first visits arrive.
  • A defined motion budget. Restrained hover states, a small loading state on the primary action, and no full page transitions.

One principle governs the whole of it. The larger the sum an investor is considering entrusting to a firm, the less they are persuaded by volume. A site prepared to say less, and to say it exceptionally well, leaves the stronger impression.

09 Accessibility

The site is built to the WCAG 2.2 AA standard. This widens the addressable audience, it is appropriate conduct for a regulated firm, and the majority of the same work is rewarded by search engines.

  • Semantic structure. Correct headings, landmarks and labels, so that assistive technology interprets the site properly.
  • Keyboard and screen reader support. Every interactive element operable without a mouse and announced correctly.
  • Colour and contrast. Text and controls meeting AA ratios in every state.
  • Forms. Clear labels, visible focus states, and informative error messages on every field.
  • Verification. Screen reader and keyboard testing conducted before launch, with the results recorded.

10 Technology platform

Your comparison document concluded that a content management system such as WordPress suits a firm seeking moderate in-house maintenance, and invited our view.

We recommend Next.js, deployed to Vercel. The concerns set out in that document are legitimate concerns regarding a conventionally coded site: manual patching, manual backups, and engineering involvement in every change. They do not arise on this platform, and the table below addresses each in turn.
