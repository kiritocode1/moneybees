### Slide 12. Our investment philosophy

- Target descriptors: undiscovered, under researched and under estimated.
- Fundamental driven: bottom-up, sector-agnostic research into core financials and business operations.
- Management leadership: understand management vision, goals and priorities; seek prudent capital allocation.
- Long-term value: invest as business owners and benefit from compounded company growth rather than react to short-term fluctuations.
- Risk/reward: evaluate value relative to price, seek margin of safety and require expected returns to justify risk.
- Outperform benchmark: aim for good returns and downside protection rather than extraordinary gains with higher capital-erosion risk.

### Slide 16. Risk management framework

- 01. Liquidity: sufficient stock trading volumes, limited price impact on entry/exit, and a reasonable cash allocation for liquidity and volatility.
- 02. Valuation: avoid purchase without adequate margin of safety; reasonable valuation is central.
- 03. Market: fundamental economic or industry events can cause risk; the stated response is patience.
- 04. Concentration: diversify across sectors/stocks and set a maximum single-stock exposure.
- The footnote directs readers to disclosure documents, PPM and key material documents, and states that securities investments are subject to market risk.
