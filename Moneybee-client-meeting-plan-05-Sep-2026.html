Method

Moneybee is focused on micro, small and medium companies. We mainly invest in brick and mortar companies, traditional businesses that we understand. Our expertise is discovering multibaggers before the market has heard of them. We focus on small companies where we can understand the downside and which have huge potential to grow. Shreyam Shah · 05 September 2026

Client wording. Terminology and multibagger claims need compliance sign-off.

Before any of this reaches the website: micro, small-cap, mid-cap and MSME are not interchangeable terms, and the decks mainly say small cap or small and mid-sized companies, so an MSME or micro mandate needs written confirmation. Brick and mortar as a formal strategy restriction also needs approval. The multibagger multiples are stated in the AIF deck but carry no purchase dates, sale dates, cost basis, treatment of corporate actions, or account attribution, so they are not publication-ready. The pyramid Shreyam refers to is the selection funnel on group profile page 13; AIF marketing deck page 11 is the multibagger list. Supporting material held for the site: 45+ years of founder experience claimed in the profile and still to be confirmed, and three case studies, KPI Green Energy, Uni Abex Alloy Products and Pitti Engineering, each needing dates, account attribution and return method.
