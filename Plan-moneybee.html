The actual problem

The gap the site has to close.

Shreyam put it plainly: people hear the Moneybee name, look it up, and land somewhere that does not match the firm behind it. A person deciding whether to hand over fifty lakh does that lookup, and the site is the only part of Moneybee they will meet before they decide.

Meanwhile the evidence that would convince them already exists. It is sitting in two PDFs marked private circulation only: a nineteen year track record, six named holdings that returned more than ten times, and a research process that starts at six thousand companies and ends at twenty.

Public pages need Suprit-approved versions of those proofs.

His clarification, 21 August

What Shreyam clarified on 21 August.

A four line follow‑up to his original reply. It is easy to skim past, and it changes the scope more than the long mail did.

01  THE BACKEND IS THEIRS

Portal, WhatsApp and CRM are theirs

“The portal is already being made and will be hosted on AWS. We will share an API with you so that clients can log in. Same with whatsapp & CRM. Your scope will only the website front.”

No backend work, no WhatsApp Business API account, no CRM build. They hand us APIs and we build against them. It also means we are now dependent on their delivery dates.

02  CONTENT IS THEIRS

They write all the content

“For Content as well, we will be ensuring end to end content of the website.”

The ₹12,000 copywriting line disappears for good. What replaces it is a scheduling risk: the site has to be built and testable before their words arrive, so every page needs a content model and a placeholder state.

03  WHERE HE WANTS US

Careers, security, compliance, brand positioning

“Careers, security, compliance and brand positioning is where we will require your help.”

Three of those four sat in the add‑on column of our proposal. He has moved them to the centre of the engagement, which is a larger brief than MB‑01 described.

⚠

One sentence is doing two jobs. “We will share an API so that clients can log in” means we implement the logged‑in screens. “Your scope will only the website front” can also mean the portal stays entirely theirs and we add a link. The plan

Seven things he asked for, and how each gets built.

In his order of priority. The left column is what he wrote, the middle is the mechanism, the strip underneath is what we already have and what we still need.

GOAL 01 · brand positioning, one of his four

Look world class

“The low hanging fruit is when you go to the website after hearing the name it should look world class.”

Three complete homepage directions are already built and running. Aditi can review the actual motion, hierarchy and responsive behaviour on the call and choose a base direction without first commissioning static mockups.

Once she picks one, we pull a design system out of it: type scale, spacing, colour roles, buttons, cards, form fields. Every page after the homepage is then assembly, not invention, which is what makes eight weeks realistic for a site this size.

The current directions use temporary imagery. A half-day shoot covering the office, team and research environment would give the selected direction original Moneybee material. This is optional and needs a yes or no.

Have:three directions, brand palette derived and contrast tested
Need:Aditi’s decision, and a yes or no on a photo shoot
GOAL 02 · his stated secondary objective

Be found, by Google and by assistants

“Create funnel of potential investors coming on the website through search engine & AI like gemini, chat gpt and claude.”

Search visibility starts with complete, crawlable HTML. Every public page will be pre-rendered, use descriptive titles and headings, and link clearly to the PMS, AIF, investment approach, team, careers and compliance pages.

Structured data will identify the organisation and its public facts. XML sitemaps, canonical URLs and registration with Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools complete the technical setup. Clear factual pages also improve the chance that AI retrieval systems can find and cite Moneybee, although no platform can guarantee citations or rankings.

The technical foundation is included in the website build. Ongoing content-led SEO can be considered later, as Shreyam requested.

Have:Next.js build, crawlable page structure and technical SEO plan
Need:approved page copy and access to the final domain’s search-console accounts

Prove the edge

Goals 04 to 07

GOAL 04 · compliance

Publish the record without getting it wrong

“We should emphasise on compliance. This works extremely well especially for NRI clients and LP's.”

A portfolio manager publishing returns in India is governed, not free. The site carries one dated set of figures, names the benchmark, names the method as time weighted, and includes the loss year instead of hiding it. Underneath sits the standard note that the numbers are not verified by the regulator.

Around that goes the disclosure architecture: SEBI registration numbers for both products in the footer, an investor grievance route, risk factors, and a regulatory page holding the full wall. For the NRI and LP audience he mentioned, eligibility and jurisdiction need saying plainly, because an overseas reader is checking whether they can invest at all before they check performance.

Suprit signs off before anything publishes. Both decks are stamped private circulation only, so clearance is a real gate, not a formality.

Have:every registration number, both benchmarks, the full figure set
Need:which figures are current, and clearance to publish
GOAL 05 · careers

Turn a thousand applicants into a pipeline

“We get over 1,000 applicants on my hiring posts on linkedin. I would like to share the website career links instead, rate the applicants here itself, and integrate it with calenders and email.”

What he described is an applicant tracking system. Built from scratch it would cost more than the entire website, so it gets split.

Phase one, in this build: role pages that are indexable and therefore useful for goal 02, an application form with CV upload, and an automatic acknowledgement. After Moneybee shortlists a candidate, an existing scheduling tool sends the calendar link so we do not build calendar software. This replaces the Google Form while preserving the review step Shreyam described.

Phase two, quoted after we see real volume: rating and pipeline stages. He offered to walk us through his current process, and that walkthrough is the cheapest way to scope it correctly.

Have:the phasing, and a route that avoids building an ATS
Need:his walkthrough, then a number
GOAL 06 · security

Answer the security question

“Security. Please let me know what is required for security purposes.”

The strongest security decision available is architectural: the marketing site stores no investor data. Forms collect a name, a contact and an enquiry, then hand off. Everything sensitive lives inside their AWS platform behind their login.

On our side that leaves a short, checkable list. The site is built from code and has no third-party plugin system to secure. TLS and automatic certificates. Firewall and denial of service protection at the edge. Content security policy and the related headers. Bot protection and rate limiting on every form so the careers page cannot be used to flood their CRM. Secrets stay in the deployment environment, never in the repository. Versioned deploys provide a direct rollback path.

Their side keeps identity, two factor, audit logging, the SEBI cyber security obligations of a licensed intermediary, and personal data handling under the DPDP Act. Worth writing that split down, because their compliance team will eventually ask who owns what.

Have:the full answer, written to forward as it stands
Need:nothing from them
GOAL 07 · their APIs

Front ends for the portal, WhatsApp and CRM

“We will share an API with you so that clients can log in. Same with whatsapp & CRM.”

All three backends are theirs and already in progress, which removes the largest technical risk from our side. What remains is interface work, and interface work is what we do.

CRM: one endpoint behind every form on the site, enquiry, distributor, careers and factsheet download, posting into their system. Small, and already absorbed into the fee. WhatsApp: we capture the opt in and link into chat; the templates and the provider relationship stay with their platform. Portal: a login in the header, and then either a hand off to their screens or screens we build, depending on which reading of his sentence is right. That is the open question set out further down.

Because their delivery now sits on our critical path, the discovery session needs both the API specification and a delivery date for each integration.

Have:confirmation the backends are theirs, which is good news
Need:endpoints, auth model, sample responses, and dates
