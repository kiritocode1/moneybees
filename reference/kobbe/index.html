Filter out the bots

Numbers you can finally trust, with every bot filtered out. Modern bots run real Chrome and rotate IPs, so they look exactly like people. Kobbe drops headless browsers, datacenter traffic, and referrer spam before they touch your stats or your quota. VPN users are untouched. How filtering works

Follow the money

See the revenue behind every page, source, and campaign. Attribution passes through checkout with Stripe, Polar, Paddle, Shopify, and friends, so your dashboard shows the money each page actually produced, not a guess. Traffic is a vanity metric; revenue isn't. Revenue attribution

Respect your visitors

No cookies, no consent banners, no raw IPs stored. Kobbe counts visits without following people around, so there is nothing to consent to and nothing to click away. Global Privacy Control and Do Not Track are honored before a single request is sent. Privacy and cookieless tracking
