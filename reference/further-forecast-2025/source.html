Design Hotels Further Forecast 2025

Design Hotels' Further Forecast 2025, Community Capital is a trend report examining how belonging and social ties are reshaping hospitality and travel. It identifies three micro-trends (Niche Appeal, New Knowledge Hubs, Longevity Networks) and uses a 2024 survey (1,196 respondents), expert interviews, and PESTE analysis to recommend hotel programs, partnerships, and experience formats that build lasting community value
