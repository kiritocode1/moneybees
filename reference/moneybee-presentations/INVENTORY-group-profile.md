## p16: RISK MANAGEMENT FRAMEWORK

- 02 VALUATION RISK: The risk of buying stocks without adequate margin of safety. Reasonable valuation is the corner stone of all our investment decision
- 03 MARKET RISK: This typically arises from certain fundamental economic conditions or events in the economy or industry. The best way to manage market risk is by being patient.
