## Correction to the brief

"Recognition" is **not** their team section. It is the awards section [O]. The team appears as the first card, "Our Team", inside a section labelled "INSIGHTS" [O]. The idea still works for Moneybee, but the source does not do it.

## Shared type system [O]

- Section eyebrow: a solid black (or white on dark) ~18px square followed by an uppercase label, 21px/700 (e.g. `■ OUR CLIENTS`). Every section except RECOGNITION uses it.
- Page H1: 145px/400, sentence case, left aligned ("About").
- Statement text: ~36-42px/400, light sans, sentence case.
- Body: 18px/400, often set in **two text columns** under a one-column lead.
- Section backgrounds alternate white and full-bleed black; hairline rules separate rows.

## 3. Naming patterns (every example verbatim)

Most labels are plain "OUR + noun". The distinctive ones are few, and they come from renaming the *job* of a section, not the section type:

| Generic section | Their name |
|---|---|
| Testimonials | KIND WORDS |
| Awards | RECOGNITION, SELECTED AWARDS |
| CSR / sustainability | GIVING BACK |
| Differentiators / why us | INSIGHTS |
| Team | Our Team (a card inside INSIGHTS) |
| R&D / blog | WonderLab® |
| Showreel | Showreel 2026 |
| Instagram | A glimpse into our world |
| Contact CTA | Have a project in mind? Get in touch. |
| Case studies | FEATURED WORK |
| Services | OUR SERVICES |
| Sectors | OUR EXPERTISE ("PRODUCT + BRAND", "FOOD + DRINK") |
| Clients | OUR CLIENTS ("Brands" / "Agencies") |
| Mission | OUR MISSION |
| Values | OUR VALUES ("Quality", "Craftsmanship", "Exploration", "Collaboration") |

Card-title patterns inside sections:
- Adjective + noun, two lines: "Detail Obsessed".
- A number as the claim: "100% Independent".
- One word emphasised in bold italic: "Creative *Service* Co".
- Single abstract nouns for values.
- Headings that set up a contrast in two short sentences: "We create impossible virtual worlds. We also invest in the real one."
- "X + Y" pairs for categories: "PRODUCT + BRAND", "CGI + motion".
- Trademarked phrases used as brand lines: "otherwise impossible stories®", "PUSH BEYOND REALITY®".

Wording habits: short declaratives, en dashes in body copy, self-deprecating framing around proof ("Awards are never the starting point"). The en dashes conflict with our no-dash rule; do not copy that habit.

## 4. Layout mechanics worth copying

1. **Square-bullet eyebrow.** ~18px solid square + 21px bold uppercase label, left aligned at the 40px page margin. One component used for every section, inverted on black. [O]
2. **Label-left, content-right split.** Eyebrow sits alone in the left third; heading and body start at ~515px (the 1/3 line) and run to the right edge (GIVING BACK, OUR SERVICES, OUR VALUES). [O]
3. **Lead + two-column body.** One large lead paragraph (~36px) over ~60% width, then 18px body split into two CSS columns beneath it. [O]
4. **Hairline row list.** Full-width rows, 1px rules, term ~42px left, one-sentence definition from the midline. Each row ~155px tall. [O]
5. **Four-up claim cards.** 4 equal columns, 2-line titles at ~42px, 18px body, no boxes or borders. [O]
6. **Logo strip + tabs.** Single row of 5-6 monochrome logos in equal cells, with text tabs at top right that switch the set. [O]
7. **Peeking slider.** Testimonials and award photos show the next item cropped at the edge (dimmed) so the reader knows it scrolls sideways. [O] Drag/arrow control not tested.
8. **White/black rhythm.** White (intro, clients, mission) → black (collage, founder, kind words, values) → white (recognition, awards, giving back) → black footer. [O]
9. **One centred section.** RECOGNITION alone breaks the left-aligned grid, centred with an uppercase H2, which makes it read as a pause. [O]
10. **Footer with live clock + marquee.** Local time ticking in grey at 58px, huge looping brand line at 178px. [O]
11. **Two image tiles as exits.** 50/50 tiles with a label bottom-left ("Our work", "Contact us") before the footer. [O]
